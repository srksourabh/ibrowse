# Code of Conduct

iBrowse exists so blind and low-vision people can use the web on equal terms. This community must be a place where they, and everyone who helps them, are welcome.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of disability, age, body size, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good faith.
- Listen to blind and low-vision users first. Their lived experience outranks our assumptions about it.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Write in plain language. Many contributors read with a screen reader or in a second language.
- Describe images, screenshots and diagrams in text when you post them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Condescension about disability, including "inspiration" framing or speaking for disabled people.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. Report problems privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form marked "conduct", or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence, and reporters are protected from retaliation.
